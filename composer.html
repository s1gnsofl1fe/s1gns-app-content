How to publish

    
    
      
  1. Load your current file (Load file, or Load live to fetch what the app sees right now).
    2. 
      
  2. Add or edit items. The preview on the right shows roughly how each looks in the app.
    3. 
      
  3. Press Validate. Fix anything red.
    4. 
      
  4. Download content.json, then upload it to your s1gns-app-content repository on github.com (Add file → Upload files → Commit changes).
    5. 
      
  5. After a few minutes, open the Vault in the app and pull down to refresh.
    6. 
    

    
The published vault list replaces the whole Vault — always publish every item you want to keep. Other sections of a loaded file (like artist) are kept exactly as they were.

    
Everything in the Vault must be free — no unlock codes, passwords or members-only items (App Store rule 3.1.1).
